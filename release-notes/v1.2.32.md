# Family Bridge 1.2.32

- New conversation form with two independent drafts: prepare with your agent, or send your own words verbatim.
- Both modes include editable context for the partner and the exact opening message. Context and message travel together as two labelled blocks, including to older peer versions.
- Agent drafts can be revised repeatedly before approval. Preparation does not publish a topic or start a conversation.
- Explicit sending saves the approved text before delivery. Retries reuse the same conversation and message; existing conversations cannot be overwritten.
- Drafts survive reloads, remain bound to the selected pair and are preserved when switching modes. The legacy new-topic draft is migrated locally.

Validation: focused preparation/delivery/retry tests, existing refinement checks, build, and isolated browser checks of both forms, persistence and scrolling. No test messages were sent to a real partner.
