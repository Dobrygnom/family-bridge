# Family Bridge 1.2.19

- Automatic connection recovery for the affected existing pair, without entering invitation codes.
- Preserves selected topics, reports, drafts, owner answers and in-progress conversations.
- Recovers undelivered and interrupted deliveries without replaying completed model work.
- Old recovered messages no longer overwrite fresh peer-version and online indicators.
- No source-chat export, plaintext recovery keys, account credentials or encryption keys are included in this release.

Validated with regression tests and an isolated live-service recovery test, including complete loss of the invited participant's old credentials and restart deduplication.
